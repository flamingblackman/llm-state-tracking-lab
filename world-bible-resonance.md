## 1. Resonance Control and Flow
A pilot must manually direct Resonance through their Aegis-Frame. Master pilots keep Resonance flowing fluidly, making their next move unpredictable to enemy sensors.

## 2. Aegis Reinforcement (Physical Buffing)
*   **Offensive Reinforcement:** Channeling Resonance into the mech drastically multiplies kinetic force.
*   **Defensive Reinforcement:** Hardening Resonance around the chassis allows the mech to shrug off attacks. If an enemy's output is higher than the defender's reinforcement, the armor shatters.

## 3. The Black Spark (Critical Phenomenon)
A spatial distortion that occurs when a pilot perfectly aligns their physical attack with a surge of Resonance. It applies an exponential multiplier (Damage to the power of 2.5).

## 4. Reverse Resonance (Positive Generation)
By taking two sources of negative Resonance and multiplying them together, a pilot creates Positive Resonance. This allows a pilot to instantly heal severed limbs and repair organic bio-muscle mid-battle. It consumes twice the normal energy.
